01 / Field Notes

My starting point for Placewise. Borrow a little of the atlas's depth while keeping this warmth and clarity.

Look & feel

Warm paper, slate ink and terracotta. A large photograph balances a calm reading column. Editorial type makes the brand feel human.

Fraunces + Manrope

Signature interaction

Click either map pin. The place and its answer stay connected. Follow-up questions deepen the same place. In the full page, the photo would settle into its map context as you scroll.

The tradeoff

The clearest balance of atmosphere and product. Keep the question visible so this feels like a tool for curiosity, rather than a travel magazine.

Latest product direction: explore an area with optional preferences and an editable group of suggested places, or ask about any point. These studies compare the single-place moment. The full landing page will demonstrate both paths.

Build direction: React, Tailwind and shadcn/ui, with Motion for layout and scroll transitions. Use MapKit JS for the actual product map, subject to the PRD's integration checks.

Design studies, not live AI or navigation. Full scroll sequences remain proposed.Placewise · September 2026
